# Vanton Neckwear Etsy Tag Selection Rules — V1.1

## Highest Principle

The model may move beyond the current market, but it must not move beyond real people and real buyer needs.

## Evidence Hierarchy

### A. Product Truth — mandatory gate
Color, material, pattern, product type, included accessories, dimensions and occasion fit must be true for the specific listing.

### B. Observed Search Terms — strong shop-specific evidence
Real Etsy Stats search terms prove that a phrase or intent has already reached this shop. They are not automatically reusable. Every observed phrase must pass relevance and IP/claim checks.

### C. Market Data — demand/competition evidence
Use searches, clicks, CTR, competition and KD to compare relevant candidate terms. Never treat search volume alone as buyer need.

### D. Validated Keywords — highest operational priority
Promote terms only after repeated shop evidence, especially listing-level clicks/orders/profit. Validated terms should outrank merely observed or market-only terms when product relevance is equal.

## 13-Tag Construction

For a normal necktie listing, aim for a balanced set rather than 13 synonyms:

- 3–4 product/core-intent tags
- 2–3 color/pattern/material tags
- 2–3 occasion/use-case tags
- 1–2 recipient/gift-intent tags
- 1–2 shop-observed long-tail tags that precisely match the item

For sets, bow ties, pocket squares, tie clips, or other accessories, replace the product/core layer with the actual product type.

## Selection Score

Use this mental priority order:

`RELEVANCE > OBSERVED SHOP EVIDENCE > VALIDATED PERFORMANCE > BUYER INTENT > MARKET CTR/SEARCH > COMPETITION ADVANTAGE`

A keyword with high volume but weak relevance loses to a precise term with lower volume.

## Phrase Handling

- Preserve useful buyer language from Etsy Stats.
- Normalize obvious typos only when building a listing; keep the raw observed phrase in the evidence file.
- Multilingual terms can be retained as evidence. Use them as tags only when they add meaningful reach and remain relevant.
- Do not force all observed phrases into the 13 tags.
- Avoid excessive duplication such as `tie`, `ties`, `mens tie`, `mens ties` in one listing unless testing intentionally.

## Hard Exclusions

Do not use a term when it is:

- irrelevant to the product;
- a competitor/third-party brand or trademark term without rights;
- an unsupported material/origin/manufacturing claim;
- a wrong product type (e.g. ascot/jabot/bow tie for a standard necktie);
- a size claim the product does not meet;
- a sustainability/upcycled/handmade claim not supported by product facts;
- a copyrighted/franchise theme without confirmed rights.

## IP / Brand Safety

Observed search terms containing third-party brands are stored as evidence but marked EXCLUDE. They must not be copied into titles, tags, descriptions or listing attributes merely because Etsy showed the listing for that query.

## Feedback Loop

After publishing:

`IMPRESSION -> CLICK -> FAVORITE/CART -> PURCHASE -> PROFIT`

Record outcomes when available. Classify evidence as:

- `MARKET`
- `OBSERVED`
- `VALIDATED`
- `EXCLUDED`

A term becomes VALIDATED only after meaningful shop-level performance evidence, not simply because it appears once in Search Terms.
