# Validated Keywords

This folder is intentionally conservative.

A keyword should **not** be called validated just because it appears in eRank or once in Etsy Search Terms.

Promote a keyword here after meaningful Vanton Neckwear performance evidence is available, preferably listing-level evidence such as repeated qualified visits, clicks, carts/favorites, orders and profit.

Suggested future columns:

`keyword | product family | listing | impressions | visits | orders | revenue | ad spend | profit | evidence window | confidence | notes`

Initial status: no terms have been promoted solely from the 2026-09-07 screenshots. Those terms remain OBSERVED until stronger performance evidence is added.
